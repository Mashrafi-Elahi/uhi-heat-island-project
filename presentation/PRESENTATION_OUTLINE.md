# Final Presentation Outline (15 slides)

1. **Title** — Urban Heat Island Mapping, team names, institution  
2. **Problem** — Urbanization, health, energy impacts  
3. **Objectives** — Hotspots, fusion, prediction, planning support  
4. **Study Area** — Map + zone types  
5. **Methodology** — Architecture diagram (field → satellite → fusion → GIS → ML → dashboard)  
6. **Field Survey** — Photos, sensor, sampling schedule  
7. **Remote Sensing** — Landsat LST + Sentinel NDVI composites  
8. **GIS Results** — Interpolated heat map screenshot  
9. **Data Fusion** — Feature table, LST–air delta analysis  
10. **ML Results** — RMSE, MAE, R², best model name  
11. **Dashboard Demo** — Live or screenshots (3 layers)  
12. **Hotspot Findings** — Top 3 hottest vs coolest zones  
13. **Environmental Causes** — Vegetation, surface, traffic  
14. **Recommendations** — Greening, cool materials, policy  
15. **Q&A** — Limitations & future IoT work
